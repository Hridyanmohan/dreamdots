GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

A few little answers

How do I order?

Use the Instagram or WhatsApp icon to message me with the piece or idea you like. I’ll confirm photos, availability, pricing, personalisation and delivery details before you decide.

Can I personalise a gift?

Share your occasion, colours, names and ideas. Options depend on the piece, and we’ll agree the details before it is made.

How long will it take?

Making time varies by design and quantity. Mention your occasion date in your enquiry so we can check your timeline together.

Are these finished product photos?

The website includes photos and illustrations as collection previews. Please ask for current product photos and available designs before placing an order.
